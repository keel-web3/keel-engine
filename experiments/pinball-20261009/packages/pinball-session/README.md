# Pinball session rules

`keel/pinball-session` owns a real `keel/pinball-arena` match. Public callers provide admitted owner buttons, signed steering and the medkit action bit. They cannot submit scores, contacts, pickups, placement, health changes or arbitrary authority commands.

Battle royale uses starting loadouts and seeded equipment crates. Solo/co-op roguelike progression remains a separate mode rule; BR crates are rejected in those modes. Solo admits one player, co-op one team of up to five, competitive teams up to five each. These roster limits do not establish a playable 100-player map, networking or device acceptance.

Score pressure uses an integer interpolated quantile of **all living players**, across every arena/layer. Dead players leave the population. The cutoff is `floor(quantile * pacePermille / 1000)`; equal scores and scores at the cutoff are safe. Zero-scoring ties and one remaining life have no pressure. Startup and sustained-deficit grace precede health damage; scoring back above the cutoff clears the warning. The default 60th percentile,65% pace,30s startup,5s deficit and2s damage interval are initial configurable tuning proposals, not approved game balance.

The `.14` authority adds bounded `equipment-damage` and `score-pressure` commands. Equipment damage passes ordinary impact invulnerability but consumes shields. Score pressure passes impact invulnerability and shields, retains the shield, and cannot claim a PvP source. The session reserves a pressure, equipment and healing queue per owner. It applies at most one command per owner each tick, with pressure before equipment before healing; overlapping damage cannot cancel score pressure. Pending commands and input edges belong to the snapshot.

Crates and healing points bind a real switch or capture mechanism in the canonical world. Only the exact current tick's actual switch/capture event admits a pickup. Crates have a seeded weighted equipment pool and one claimant. Medkits are finite, use press edges and a120-tick cooldown, and heal through an admitted command. Healing points have independent bounded owner cooldowns. There is no visual crate or healing-point renderer yet.

`readHealingPointStates(ownerId)` exposes copied, frozen physical bindings, last claim ticks, remaining cooldown and living-owner availability for in-world lamps/effects. It reads canonical completed ticks and saved healing records; sampling it never advances or mutates the session. Rendering can explain readiness without inventing a separate timer.

Passing damage uses two consecutive actual body positions, relative swept distance, active state, minimum speed, opposing teams, arena/layer/elevation, transport exclusion and solid rail/gate/bumper/flipper sight checks. Portal, ramp, fall and capture transitions cannot form damage sweeps across unrelated spaces. Impact equipment requires an actual ball-contact event. Equipment definitions are replaceable bounded recipes; appearance or NFT ownership grants no equipment through this module.

Snapshot restore builds and validates a candidate authority before committing any session state. Saves bind world, current authority, exact rules/catalogue/loadouts and effective roster/options. Bounds and consistency checks catch malformed local saves; ranked authenticity still requires the host's authoritative transcript. Public frame/portable arena replay preserve the new damage causes and equipment source attribution.

`createSessionReplayRecorder` takes exclusive ownership of session stepping and an exact world/rules/options descriptor. It journals four bytes per owner per tick: native buttons, signed quantized X/Y steering and session action bits. `encodeSessionReplay`/`decodeSessionReplay` use bounded binary input/snapshot sections, a checksummed header and the exact embedded generated world. The default rolling segment holds three minutes; rollover retains the complete session snapshot, including claimed crates, held medkit input edges and pending damage/healing. `createSessionReplayPlayer` owns a separate full session and recomputes every mode effect from controls. It verifies the final complete session snapshot, rather than trusting recorded damage or crate events. Exported metadata/bytes and player inputs are copied. This local checksum verifies consistency, not a host signature or ranked authenticity.

This module is a locally tested foundation. It does not implement landing-area character combat, NPC movement/defeat, crate art, input UI, roguelike group drafts, end-to-end networking or physical-device gameplay. Those stay explicit delivery gates.
